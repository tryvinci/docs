A clip that starts from a still keeps the product that you approved. A clip from words only can change the product in every frame. This guide starts from a still on the **Image** page and makes a 6-second clip with one slow camera move on the **Video** page.

## What you need

- The free and open source Vinci studio, with live spend on (the install is in step 1 of the [Image guide](/learn/four-stills-from-one-prompt))
- A model provider key (this example uses Wavespeed)
- A still that you like: make one on the Image page, or use your own picture

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from a still">
    On the **Image** page, hover the still and click **Animate**. The **Video** page opens, with the still as the **Start** frame. You can also drop your own picture on the start frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the move, not the picture">
    The still already shows the product. Write only what moves: the camera, and one or two small things in the scene. The example prompt:
```text
Slow push-in on the kettle. The steam from the cup drifts up. Nothing else moves.
```
    "Nothing else moves" helps to keep the product as it is in the still.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Click **Change** and pick a model. The form then shows only the lengths, shapes and sizes that the model takes. The example uses **Hailuo 2.3 Fast Pro**, which makes 6-second clips only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the length and the shape">
    Pick the length under **Duration**. Under **Aspect**, pick the shape of the still: 16:9 in the example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the price, then generate">
    The price is on the **Generate** button: $0.330 for Hailuo 2.3 Fast Pro. Click **Generate** one time. The clip shows in **History** when the model is done, after 1 to 2 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the clip to the last frame">
    Play the clip to the end, and look at the product in the last frame: the shape, the label and the color. If the product changes, ask for a smaller move, or try a different model.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **One move in each clip.** Ask for one push-in, one pan or one tilt. A small, slow move keeps the product steady.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The size of the clip can be different from the size on the **Quality** chip. In the example, the chip showed 1920×1080, and the clip came back at 1364×768. Check the size of the clip before you put it in an edit.
</Note>

## Before you use a clip

- [ ] The product in the last frame matches the still
- [ ] The clip has one move, and the move is slow
- [ ] The clip has the shape of the still

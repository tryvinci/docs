You can make a short product ad from a single good photo. Shoot it plainly, generate a few scenes around it, add one camera move and cut it to fifteen seconds.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="What you get" icon="flag-checkered">
    A 15 second vertical product ad.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Time to read" icon="clock">
    About 4 minutes. Each step works with any toolset.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What you need

- One clean photo of your product
- A background remover
- An image model and an image-to-video model

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shoot one clean photo">
    Put the product against a plain wall near a window and take the shot. Then run it through a background remover so you have a clean cutout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a few scenes">
    Ask an image model for three scenes, for example a studio shot, a lifestyle setting and a label close-up. Tell it to keep your product exactly as it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one slow camera move">
    Turn each scene into a five second clip with a single slow move, like a push-in. Simple motion keeps the product steady.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write three short lines">
    A hook for the first two seconds, one benefit, one action. Keep each line short enough to read at a glance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut it vertical to fifteen seconds">
    Put your best clip first, add captions and export at 9:16.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Best clip first.** The opening second decides whether people stay. Lead with the shot that looks most finished.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  **Avoid this: melted labels.** Some clips come back with the label warped. Do not try to fix them in the edit. Regenerate those shots.
</Warning>
